Our Campus Landmark & Statue

Located in Fiapre near Sunyani, Notre Dame Girls SHS stands as a beacon of female education in the Bono Region of Ghana.

Our iconic campus statue stands proudly on our green grounds, representing faith, guidance, and our unwavering commitment to moulding disciplined future leaders.

WAEC / WASSCE Curriculum

Academic Programs

Comprehensive course outlines across our five core academic disciplines.

STEM Department

1. General Science

Prepares students for university majors in Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, Computer Science, and Data Analytics.

Elective Courses Offered:

Physics, Chemistry, Elective Mathematics, Biology, and Computing.

Humanities

2. General Arts

Builds essential writing, debate, and analytical skills for Law, Political Science, Journalism, and Public Policy.

Elective Courses Offered:

Literature-in-English, Economics, Geography, History, Government, and CRS.

Business & Commerce

3. Business

Trains students in accounting principles, financial management, business management, and micro/macroeconomics.

Elective Courses Offered:

Financial Accounting, Cost Accounting, Business Management, Economics, and Elective Mathematics.

Applied Sciences

4. Home Economics

Practical training in food processing, nutritional science, family resource management, and apparel design.

Elective Courses Offered:

Food and Nutrition, Management-in-Living, Clothing and Textiles, and Biology.

Creative Arts

5. Visual Arts

Fosters studio production skills, digital graphics, spatial design, and fine arts for university degrees in Architecture and Design.

Elective Courses Offered:

General Knowledge in Art, Graphic Design, Sculpture, Textiles, and Picture Making.
